## 2. Standalone Production Prompt (Style 2A: Modern Studio Tech)

Copy and paste this prompt directly into your video generation model (Gemini Omni Flash, Luma, Runway, etc.):

```text
A minimalist 2D animated stick figure wearing a bright red beanie (smooth knit, no pom-pom) and a yellow t-shirt with simple black stick limbs, alongside a smaller plain black-and-white minimalist stick figure, in a modern bright white studio space with subtle light-gray perspective grid lines on the floor plane. High-key studio lighting, clean white negative space, sleek glowing cyan and electric blue glass holographic UI elements. Smooth 2D animation style.

Timed Synchronized Visual Beats:
- [0–3s] (VO: "En el colegio, dos jugadores acaparan todo el juego dejando a los demás en la sombra..."): The red-beanie stick figure leaps into the air over a high white net to violently smash a volleyball, yelling aggressively while a glowing red holographic label reading "47.9% EXCLUDED" hovers over the smaller stick figure who huddles timidly on the left.
- [3–7s] (VO: "¡Hasta que el voleibol sentado baja la red y obliga la pelvis al suelo!"): An official whistle tweets as the net mechanically drops down to 1.15 meters. The red-beanie figure tries to leap again, but is immediately pulled to the floor, landing seated flat on his pelvis with a funny reaction; a glowing cyan floor ring illuminates under his pelvis reading "PELVIS ON GROUND: Fy = 0".
- [7–10s] (VO: "El cuerpo obliga a cooperar. En la cancha del San Bonifacio, jugamos todos."): Seated on the floor, the smaller stick figure slides swiftly on his arms and executes a clean forearm pass; the red-beanie stick figure returns the pass with a smile, and both enthusiastically slap a seated high-five as a luminous emerald-green banner appears reading "EL SAQUE DE RECONCILIACIÓN — 71.2%".

Audio voiceover only, strictly no speech bubbles, no dialogue text boxes.
Spoken voiceover script: "En el colegio, dos jugadores acaparan todo el juego dejando a los demás en la sombra... ¡Hasta que el voleibol sentado baja la red y obliga la pelvis al suelo! El cuerpo obliga a cooperar. En la cancha del San Bonifacio, jugamos todos."

Negative constraints: no photorealistic human skin or faces, no 3D humanoid CGI uncanny valley models, no chaotic line glitches, no speech bubbles, no dialogue text boxes, STRICTLY MINIMALIST, NO CIRCUIT BOARD TEXTURES, NO SCI-FI WALL PANELS, NO CRACKED CONCRETE.
```

## 3. Versión Alternativa (Style 1: Classic Minimalist Dark)

```text
Use minimalist two-dimensional white line animation on a flat, uniform, pitch-black canvas. Show two consistent stick figures with hollow circular heads, no faces, no eyes, no mouths, no hair, no clothing, no filled bodies, stable proportions, connected line limbs, and uniform medium line weight. Exactly two arms and two legs are visible per figure. The figures stay white; vivid red, electric cyan, and warm gold accent colors affect only props and graphical overlays.

Timed Synchronized Visual Beats:
- [0–3s] (VO: "En el colegio, dos jugadores acaparan todo el juego dejando a los demás en la sombra..."): A tall white stick figure leaps across a 2.43m white line net to spike a glowing ball, while a smaller white stick figure on the sideline shrinks under a sharp red angular shape displaying "47.9%".
- [3–7s] (VO: "¡Hasta que el voleibol sentado baja la red y obliga la pelvis al suelo!"): The net line rapidly drops to 1.15m. Both stick figures sit flat on the bottom baseline; a pulsing electric-cyan circle locks around their seated base with the cota "Fy = 0". The tall figure reaches out to pass instead of smash.
- [7–10s] (VO: "El cuerpo obliga a cooperar. En la cancha del San Bonifacio, jugamos todos."): The smaller stick figure slides on its arm lines, bumping the ball in an arc to the other figure; they score together and clasp hands in a warm gold circle of light.

Audio voiceover only, strictly no speech bubbles, no dialogue boxes.
Spoken voiceover script: "En el colegio, dos jugadores acaparan todo el juego dejando a los demás en la sombra... ¡Hasta que el voleibol sentado baja la red y obliga la pelvis al suelo! El cuerpo obliga a cooperar. En la cancha del San Bonifacio, jugamos todos."

Negative constraints: no gray tint, no paper texture, no gradients, no shadows, no lighting, no bloom, no fog, no 3D depth, no speech bubbles, no dialogue text boxes.
```
